    How the grid becomes music

    Nuclear → low drone
    A steady bass tone. It barely moves, like the plants.
    Hydro → flowing pad
    Warm, slowly swelling chords. Louder when Niagara and St. Lawrence run high.
    Wind → breath and bells
    Air through a filter, with high bells that ring more often as wind output rises.
    Solar and other renewables → plucks
    They play the melody. More output, more notes.
    Gas and dual fuel → saws and brass
    A buzzing drone and short brass stabs. They get harsher and add a clashing note as the clean share drops.
    Clean share → key
    Above about 54% clean the piece plays in a bright major key. In the middle it plays Dorian. Below about 46% it turns to a tense Phrygian.
    Demand → tempo
    A soft heartbeat on every beat. Statewide load sets its speed, from roughly 60 to 135 beats per minute.
    Zone strain → color
    On the map, each zone warms toward red as it nears its highest load in the archive.
